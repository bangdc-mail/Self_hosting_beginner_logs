# Self-hosting as a beginner

## Introduction

This is a personal log of my journey into self-hosting — a beginner’s attempt at learning how to take control of their own digital infrastructure. I'm writing this mainly as a record for myself: to track what I’ve tried, what I’ve learned (or misunderstood), and how things evolve over time. It’s part diary, part study notes, and part “please let me remember how I fixed that weird issue.”

That said, I’m also sharing this publicly in case it helps others who are thinking of starting their own self-hosting adventure. If you're new to this too, maybe you’ll find something useful here — or at least relatable.

Please keep in mind: **this is not a definitive guide**. I am by no means an expert or a master yet, and some of what I write may be incomplete, inaccurate, or just plain wrong. Please always double check or do some fact checking whenever you need.

Above all, I hope this log makes your own exploration a little easier, or at least more fun. Let’s see where this rabbit hole leads.
